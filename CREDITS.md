## Tarot artwork

Asset preparation by **Luciella Elisabeth Scarlett**, also known as **Luciella Scarlett / LuciellaES**.

Source: [Rider-Waite Smith Tarot Cards (CC0)](https://luciellaes.itch.io/rider-waite-smith-tarot-cards-cc0).

The source pack contains scans of the original Rider-Waite Smith deck obtained from Wikipedia. LuciellaES cleaned up and resized the scans and added a black border. The card images used here have been converted to WebP.

The source page identifies the original illustrations as public domain under the creator's assessment of Australian copyright law, and explicitly releases the included card-back design under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). It asks users to assess the original illustrations' status for their own country and use case. Attribution to LuciellaES is optional; it is included here to acknowledge the asset preparation.

The software's MIT license does not replace the artwork's source terms. The default text-and-CSS card back in this app is separate from the source pack's card-back design.
